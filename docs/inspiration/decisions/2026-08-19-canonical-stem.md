## The decision

`mkt-01` is:

> Explain how target markets affect e-marketing, people, processes and physical evidence.

Everything authored on it — the four required concepts, the twelve pathways, the
component guidance and the help ladders — is built around the four elements this
wording fixes. Decoding, plain English, thesis guidance and comparison answers all
read this string.

The broader question:

> Explain how target markets influence the development of marketing strategies.

is a genuinely different, less constrained task. It keeps its own id, `mkt-04`,
with no authored pathways. It is **not** an alias.

## Why

Without this rule the new learning layer could quietly broaden a constrained
question: a student would be decoded and taught against four fixed elements while
the stem in front of them invited any element of the mix.

## The general rule

All question decoding, plain-English explanation, thesis guidance and comparison
answers use the **exact live exam stem** as canonical, never a generic matched
practice-question text.
