---
title:   A model answer may be shown when asked for, never inserted
date:    2026-08-19
status:  active
governs: the learning layers, Compare, the help ladder
supersedes: the earlier blanket rule that the student's own context is never modelled
---

## The decision

Guided mode may show an authored, exact-context model answer — a thesis, a
paragraph — when the student explicitly asks to see one. It must never be
inserted into their draft, and it must never appear unrequested.

## What does not change

The **sentence ladder** keeps its original rule. L5 stays a
`differentContextExample`: a fully written sentence is only safe because it is set
somewhere else. The relaxation applies to the learning layers, not to the rung
that sits under the line being written.

## Why

The earlier rule protected ownership to the point where a student could be
coached on phrasing an idea they did not understand. Withholding the thing we are
supposed to be teaching is not independence.

> Guided mode should never force a student to remain ignorant in order to
> preserve independence. It should make the correct content, reasoning and
> acceptable response models easy to access, while keeping the act of
> constructing the response with the student.

## The three cases, kept distinct

1. giving the answer automatically — never
2. making it available as teaching content on request — yes
3. requiring the student to understand, select, adapt and write their own
   response afterwards — the point of guided mode
