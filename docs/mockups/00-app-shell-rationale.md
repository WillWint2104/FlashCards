# 00 · The shared app shell: reading key

**Status: proposed, awaiting the owner's approval.** Nothing is implemented. No app
code, test, or frozen drawing changed. These drawings answer one brief: one
consistent Marginal frame around the pages that already exist, in the visual
language of the Essay practice bar. They are a shell migration, not a redesign.

## What the drawings are

They are generated by `00-app-shell.build.mjs`. It opens the real app (the
walkthrough build that `tests/mkwalk.py` makes from the committed
`marginal-preview.html`) at 1440×900 with the real Fredoka and Nunito, and goes to
each page the way a student does.

From each page it removes only the old chrome:

- the static `<header>`, with its hard-coded Economics unit and topic;
- the pill switcher (`div.nav`);
- the signed-in row (`.whoami`), where there is one;
- on Essay practice, the old `header.qp-nav`, and the old header, pill and Study
  page that the app leaves underneath that overlay.

It inserts the one bar and, on Essay practice, the area's own row at the top of the
page. It restyles nothing in a page. The one drawing aid makes the Essay practice
overlay static, so its drawing scrolls as a page.

It then proves three things, and stops if any fails:

1. **The page under the bar is unchanged.** Every content element keeps its left,
   width and height, and moves by one vertical offset, the change in chrome. The
   Essay practice footer is checked as its own group, because the overlay keeps it
   at the bottom of the screen until the page outgrows the screen.
2. **The static file renders as the page did.** Every element's box (left, top,
   width, height) is identical, and every pixel is identical.
3. **There is one bar.** Its geometry is the same on every page, and it stays one
   row at 1280px.

## The bar, for implementation

These are the values the drawings use. The build checks that the bar is identical
on every page.

  - Inner column 1320px maximum, with 28px side padding (1264px of content).
  - 16px between the brand, the links group and the right-hand group.
  - Height 67.4px, the Essay practice picker bar's own height (measured there at
    67.39px).
  - At 1280px wide it is the same height and still one row.
- **Brand.**
  - A 30px `#12B27A` tile with a white Fredoka "M" at 16px.
  - Then "Marginal" in Fredoka 700 at 18px, −0.3px tracking, `#18272D`.

    `aria-current="page"`. The underline is authored at 2.5px, as in the source,

    16px of padding and 2px of border.
  - Hover is `#18272D`.
  - Each link reserves its bold width, so the current section's weight never

## Where each part of the brief lands

- **One shell, from the Essay practice bar.** The frame, brand, link type,
  underline and quiet button are the same as `.qp-nav`, `.qp-brand`,
  `.qp-navlink` and `.es-util.quiet`. The deliberate differences are choices 2 to
  6 below.
- **Global navigation.** Study, Create, Test mode, Essay practice. The old pill
  and the old header are gone from every page, and the hard-coded Economics unit
  and topic appear nowhere.
- **My essays belongs to Essay practice.** It is one of that area's two pages,
  below the bar, not a peer of Test mode.
- **Subject context comes from the open workspace, by key.** It is display only,
  with no stored "current subject" and no second source of truth. It is read
  through the subject registry the app already uses, `esAllSubjects().subjects`:
  the shipped packages plus any imported subjects. That is the same object
  `tmPackages()` and `esSubjectContent()` read. The walkthrough imports none, so
  the drawings read the shipped packages. Page by page:
  - **Study:** "Economics · Year 12".
    - Study has no key today. `content.js` says only `subject: "Economics"`, and
      Year 12 lives on the Economics package.
    - The proposal is one declared key, `CONTENT.subjectKey = "economics"`, read
      through the registry like any other. It is a display pointer only: marking
      keeps reading what it reads now.
    - It must never be a match on the label, which is the inference the Gate 3
      audit removed.
    - A gate test should assert that the key's package label equals
      `CONTENT.subject`. Today only this build checks it.
  - **Create:** nothing. A flashcard set declares no subject.
  - **Test mode, library and import:** nothing. No paper is open, and decisions 4
    and 18 and the Page 1 note say the library names no subject.
  - **Test mode, one paper open** (overview, sitting, results, review):
    - The paper's own key, resolved through `resolveAuthority`, gives the
      package's label and stage: "Business Studies · Year 12". The file's "Stage
      6" is its claim; the package's stage is what resolves (02-import
      rationale).
    - A paper whose key does not resolve shows **no** context. The bar must not
      inherit `tmSubjectOf`'s fallback to the file's course text.
  - **Test mode, a question-type practice over several papers:**
    - Proposed: the bar names a subject only if every item resolves to the same
      one, and then on every page of that practice.
    - Today's header is not consistent here:
      - the type overview and the submit page already use that rule;
      - the sitting and the single-question review show each question's own
        paper, so a mixed practice changes subject from question to question;
      - results shows the first item's.
    - The bar should not inherit those.
  - **Essay practice:**
    - The package of the subject the page is on, label and stage, as `.qp-subj`
      shows on the picker pages today.
    - Inside a draft, it is the draft's own subject (`esAttemptSubject`). That
      adds the stage, which today's writing bar leaves out.
    - The build checks that the bar says exactly what the two drawn pages' current
      header says.
- **Signed-in identity and Sign out** move into the bar on every page.
  - Today they are a row under the pill on the Study home and the Test mode
    library only, and nowhere in Essay practice.
  - The data is `Cloud.who()`: class code and student number, the only identity
    the profile holds.
  - Sign out does what it does now.

## Choices made here, open to change on review

1. **"Study" or "Home": yours to decide.**
   - The drawings say "Study". Study is Marginal's home page today, the brand
     already goes there, and one link per destination avoids a Home link and a
     Study link that do the same thing.
   - Your brief wrote "Home / Study".
   - ui57 holds an earlier Essay practice decision: every full page offers Home,
     as a control of its own and not only the wordmark (`#eshome`). The "Study"
     link meets that intent, a visible control to Marginal's home, but not its
     wording.
   - If it should read "Home", only the label changes.
2. **Contrast fixed where the source fell short.**

   | Element | Source colour | Ratio | Colour here | Ratio |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | Context and account text, on white | `#95A7AD` (`.qp-subj`) | 2.50:1 | `#63787F` | 4.64:1 |
   | Focus rings, on white | `#12B27A` | 2.74:1 | `#0E9765` | 3.73:1 |
   | Links below the bar, on `#F6F9F9` | `#63787F` | 4.39:1 | `#5D7178` | 4.84:1 |
   | Demo badge | | 2.95:1 | `#0E7A4E` on `#E7F8F0` | 4.88:1 |

   - The links and Sign out already used `#63787F` in the source.
   - A focus indicator needs 3:1, hence the darker ring.
   - The green underline keeps `#12B27A`, as in the source. The current section is
     also shown by weight, by colour (15.36:1) and by `aria-current`.
3. **Bold width reserved.** With the source's CSS, the current link's weight
   moves the links after it by about 0.7 to 1.1px, depending on which link is
   current, so the bar would shift from page to page. Each link now holds its bold
   width.
4. **An area's own pages sit in the page, not in a second bar,** so the bar is
   the same object, at the same height, on every page.
5. **The account is "class code · student number" with a user icon.** There are
   no names in the profile, and the old row's "Signed in:" label is not needed
   once the account sits in the bar.
6. **The bar is sticky.**
   - The Essay practice picker bar scrolls away with its page.
   - The app's own header and the Essay practice writing bar are sticky.
   - One bar for every page has to pick one, and these drawings pick sticky, so
     the sections and Sign out are always within reach.

## Still open, for the owner

1. **Content width.**
   - The bar's content is 1264px wide. Study, Create and Test mode keep their
     1180px page column (1100px of content).
   - So at 1440px their content starts at x=170 while the bar's starts at x=88.
     The Essay practice pages line up with the bar.
   - Lining the rest up means either widening those pages, including the frozen
     Test mode layouts, or narrowing the bar and with it the Essay practice pages.
   - That is its own decision, so these drawings change neither.
2. **Page ground.** Study, Create and Test mode sit on `#EEF3F5`, Essay practice
   on `#F6F9F9`. Unchanged here.
3. **Two stage words on one Test mode screen.**
   - On the paper overview, the frozen page's kicker reads "Business Studies ·
     Stage 6", the file's claim (02-import).
   - The bar reads "Year 12", the package's stage, as the brief asks.
   - Both are true for NSW, but a student sees two different words.
   - The options are a subject-only bar in Test mode, or reopening the kicker on
     the frozen page.
4. **Mobile.**
   - The old header, the pill and the Essay practice bar are the same markup at
     every width. The Essay practice bar folds into a More menu below 860px.
   - The proposal keeps phones as they are until the mobile pass: the new bar
     applies above 860px, and the current narrow-screen chrome stays below it.
   - The alternative is a narrow version of the new bar now, which is the mobile
     work the brief defers.
5. **Surfaces not drawn:**
   - the Test mode sitting, with its own bar and footer (08);
   - the Essay practice writing surface, with its Learn, Notebook, mode switch
     and Exit essay;
   - the sign-in page: brand only, or the full bar.
6. **Footer.** The Essay practice pages keep their footer; the others have none.
7. **Window title.** `index.html`'s title still joins "Marginal" and the
   Economics unit with an em dash, on every page. It should follow the area, for
   example "Marginal · Test mode".
8. **Em dashes in page copy.** Some Study and Create text, such as the Settings
   heading, joins its words with an em dash. That copy is the app's own and is
   captured as it is; it is not swept here.
