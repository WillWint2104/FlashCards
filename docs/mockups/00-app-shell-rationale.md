# 00 · The shared app shell: reading key

**Status: proposed, awaiting the owner's approval.** Nothing is implemented. No app
code, test, or frozen drawing changed. These drawings answer one brief: one
consistent Marginal frame around the pages that already exist, in the visual
language of the Essay practice bar. They are a shell migration, not a redesign.

## What the drawings are

They are generated by `00-app-shell.build.mjs`, which opens the real app (the
walkthrough build that `tests/mkwalk.py` makes from the committed
`marginal-preview.html`) at 1440×900 with the real Fredoka and Nunito, and goes to
each page the way a student does. From each page it removes only the old chrome:

- the static `<header>`, with its hard-coded "HSC Economics / Distribution of
  Income & Wealth";
- the pill switcher (`div.nav`);
- on Essay practice, the old `header.qp-nav`, and the old header, pill and Study
  page left underneath that overlay.

The build stops if it would remove anything else. Then it adds the one bar, writes
the page as a static file with the stylesheet rules that page uses, and checks
the file renders as the live page did. So what sits under the bar is the app as it
is today, not a redrawing of it.

## The bar, for implementation

Every number below is measured in the drawings, and the build fails if any page
differs from Study.

- **Frame.** White, 1px `#E8EEEF` bottom border, sticky to the top. Inner column
  1320px maximum with 28px side padding (1264px of content), 16px gaps. Height
  67.4px, the Essay practice picker bar's own height (13px padding around a 40.4px
  link). At 1280px wide it is the same height and still one row.
- **Brand.** A 30px `#12B27A` tile with a white Fredoka "M" at 16px, then
  "Marginal" in Fredoka 700 at 18px, −0.3px tracking, `#18272D`. Hover turns the
  word `#0E9765`. The brand is the route to Study, Marginal's home.
- **Sections.** Study, Create, Test mode, Essay practice. Nunito 600 14px
  `#63787F`, 8px 10px padding, 4px apart, 14px after the brand. The current
  section is 700 `#18272D` with a 2.5px `#12B27A` underline and
  `aria-current="page"`. Hover is `#18272D`. Each link reserves its bold width,
  so the current section's weight never moves its neighbours: the links sit at
  the same x on every page.
- **Right side.** The open workspace's subject and year (13px 600 `#63787F`), a
  1px `#E8EEEF` divider, the account (a user icon, then class code · student
  number, 13px 600 `#63787F`), and Sign out, styled like Essay practice's quiet
  buttons: no border, log-out icon, and on hover `#FAFCFC` with a `#E8EEEF` border
  and the icon in `#0E9765`. With no subject, the subject and divider are left out
  and the account stays where it is.
- **Focus.** A 2px `#0E9765` ring, offset 2px, on the brand, every link and Sign
  out.
- **An area's own pages.** Essay practice's two pages, Essay practice and My
  essays, are a row inside the page, directly under the bar and aligned to the
  content: 14px links in `#5D7178`, the current one 700 `#18272D` with the same
  green underline, over a 1px `#E8EEEF` rule. The bar itself shows "Essay
  practice" as the current section on both.

At 1440px the brand's tile starts at x=88, the content edge, and the links at
x=233.6, 295.6, 362.7 and 454.1. Sign out's text ends at x=1351, 1px (the quiet
button's border) inside the other content edge.

## Where each part of the brief lands

- **One shell, from the Essay practice bar.** Same frame, brand, link type,
  underline and quiet button as `.qp-nav`, `.qp-brand`, `.qp-navlink` and
  `.es-util.quiet`. The differences are listed under the choices below.
- **Global navigation.** Study, Create, Test mode, Essay practice. The old pill
  and the old header are gone from every page. "HSC Economics / Distribution of
  Income & Wealth" appears nowhere.
- **My essays belongs to Essay practice.** It is one of that area's own two
  pages, below the bar, not a peer of Test mode.
- **Subject context comes from the open workspace, by key.** It is display only,
  read through the same registry the app already uses (`window.ESSAY.subjects`),
  with no stored "current subject" and no second source of truth:
  - **Study:** "Economics · Year 12". Study has no key today: `content.js` says
    only `subject: "Economics"`, and Year 12 lives on the Economics package. The
    proposal is one declared key, `CONTENT.subjectKey = "economics"`, read through
    the registry like any other. It must never be a match on the label, which is
    the inference the Gate 3 audit removed. The build checks that the proposed
    key's package names the subject Study shows.
  - **Create:** nothing. A flashcard set declares no subject.
  - **Test mode, library and import:** nothing. No paper is open, and decisions 4
    and 18 and the Page 1 note say the library names no subject.
  - **Test mode, a paper open** (overview, sitting, results, review): the paper's
    own key through `resolveAuthority`, so the package's label, plus the package's
    stage, "Year 12". The file's "Stage 6" is its claim; the package's stage is
    what resolves (02-import rationale). A practice drawing on papers of two
    subjects names none, as the Test mode header does today.
  - **Essay practice:** the package of the subject the page is on, label and
    stage, as `.qp-subj` shows today. Inside a draft, the draft's own subject
    (`esAttemptSubject`). The build checks the bar says exactly what the page's
    current header says.
- **Signed-in identity and Sign out** move into the bar on every page. Today they
  are a row under the pill on the Study home and the Test mode library only, and
  nowhere in Essay practice. The data is `Cloud.who()` (class code and student
  number, the only identity the profile holds), and Sign out does what it does
  now. When cloud sign-in is not configured, the account and Sign out are left out.

## Choices made here, open to change on review

1. **"Study", not "Home".** Study is Marginal's home page today, the brand
   already goes there, and the Essay practice bar's own Home button would then
   duplicate both. That gives one link per destination.
2. **Contrast fixed where the source fell short.** The source's `.qp-subj` grey
   `#95A7AD` is 2.50:1 on white, so context and account text are `#63787F`
   (4.64:1). The links and Sign out already used `#63787F`. Focus rings are
   `#0E9765` (3.73:1), because `#12B27A` is 2.74:1, below the 3:1 a focus
   indicator needs. Links below the bar are `#5D7178`: `#63787F` is 4.39:1 on the
   `#F6F9F9` page ground, and `#5D7178` is 4.84:1. The green underline keeps
   `#12B27A` as in the source; the current section is also shown by weight, by
   colour (15.36:1) and by `aria-current`.
3. **Bold width reserved.** With the source's CSS, the current link's weight
   moves the links after it by about 0.7px, so the bar would shift from page to
   page. Each link now holds its bold width.
4. **An area's own pages sit in the page, not in a second bar,** so the bar is
   the same object, at the same height, on every page.
5. **The account is "class code · student number" with a user icon.** There are
   no names in the profile, and the old row's "Signed in:" label is not needed
   once the account sits in the bar.

## Still open, for the owner

1. **Content width.** The bar's content is 1264px wide. Study, Create and Test
   mode keep their 1180px page column (1100px of content), so at 1440px their
   content starts at x=170 while the bar's starts at x=88. The Essay practice
   pages line up with the bar. Lining the rest up means widening those pages,
   including the frozen Test mode layouts, or narrowing the bar and with it the
   Essay practice pages. That is its own decision, so these drawings change
   neither.
2. **Page ground.** Study, Create and Test mode sit on `#EEF3F5`, Essay practice
   on `#F6F9F9`. Unchanged here.
3. **Two stage words on one Test mode screen.** On the paper overview, the frozen
   page's kicker reads "Business Studies · Stage 6", the file's claim (02-import),
   while the bar reads "Year 12", the package's stage, as the brief asks. Both
   are true for NSW, but a student sees two different words. The options are a
   subject-only bar in Test mode, or reopening the kicker on the frozen page.
4. **Surfaces not drawn:**
   - the Test mode sitting, with its own bar and footer (08);
   - the Essay practice writing surface, with its Learn, Notebook, mode switch
     and Exit essay;
   - the sign-in page: brand only, or the full bar;
   - narrow screens, which are out of scope (desktop only).
5. **Footer.** The Essay practice pages keep their footer; the others have none.
6. **Window title.** `index.html` still says "Marginal — Distribution of Income &
   Wealth", with an em dash and the Economics unit, on every page. It should
   follow the area, for example "Marginal · Test mode".
7. **Em dashes in page copy.** Study and Create text such as "Settings — AI essay
   grading" is the app's own and is captured as it is; it is not swept here.

## Checked

- **Fonts:** Fredoka and Nunito proven loaded on every page.
- **Regeneration:** generated with `node docs/mockups/00-app-shell.build.mjs`,
  and regenerated byte-identical with `MOCKUP_OUT`.
- **Chrome removal:** only the old chrome was removed, asserted per page.
- **One bar:** the same height, brand, links and Sign out on all six pages, and
  one row at 1280px.
- **Each drawing renders as its live page:**
  - every element's box is identical;
  - the pixels are identical too, except on Essay practice: 330 pixels (0.02%)
    of sub-pixel shading on two icon tiles in the right-hand column, where the
    app composites a layer the static file does not.
- **Contrast**, white unless stated:

  | Text or indicator | Colour | Ratio |
  |---|---|---|
  | Links, context, account, Sign out | `#63787F` | 4.64:1 |
  | Current section | `#18272D` | 15.36:1 |
  | Sign out on hover, on `#FAFCFC` | `#18272D` | 14.92:1 |
  | Links below the bar, on `#F6F9F9` | `#5D7178` | 4.84:1 |
  | Focus ring | `#0E9765` | 3.73:1 |

- **Style:** no em dash in the shell, the titles, the states sheet or this note.
- **Errors:** no page errors.
- **Footer test:** no new file contains `class="footer"`, so t34's count of 14 is
  unaffected.
