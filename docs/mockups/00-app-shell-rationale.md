# 00 · The shared app shell: reading key

**Status: approved by the owner with the decisions below, and implemented.** These
drawings are the approved design. The implementation (index.html, app.js,
content.js) draws the same bar from one renderer, and ui78 holds it to these
drawings. It is a shell migration, not a redesign: the pages under the bar keep
their layouts.

## Decisions the owner locked

1. **Study, not Home.** The areas are `Study | Create | Test mode | Essay practice`.
   The brand also returns to Study.
2. **My essays stays Essay practice's own page,** in the area's row under the bar
   (`Essay practice | My essays`). It is not a fifth area.
3. **Content widths stay as they are.** The bar can sit on a wider grid than a
   page. Study, Create and Test mode keep their 1180px column.
4. **The bar is sticky.**
5. **The darker, accessible colours and focus rings stay** (contrast table below).
6. **The subject is the most specific authoritative context the open workspace
   supplies,** and display only:

   | Open workspace | The bar says |
   |---|---|
   | Study | Economics · Year 12, the course Study's content declares (`CONTENT.subjectKey`) |
   | A set the student made | nothing |
   | Create | nothing |
   | Test mode library and import | nothing |
   | A Test mode paper | Business Studies · Stage 6: the subject its own key resolves to, and the stage the paper declares |
   | Essay practice and My essays | Business Studies · Year 12, the chosen course's package |
   | An essay being written | the attempt's own package, whatever the picker is moved to |

   Stage 6 is not converted to Year 12, so the paper overview's kicker and the bar
   now agree. Nothing stores a "current subject", and nothing outside the shell
   reads what the bar shows.
7. **Widths.**
   - 1024px and up: the whole bar.
   - 861px to 1023px: the same bar without the subject. The brand, the four areas,
     the account and Sign out stay, on one row.
   - 860px and below: no designed treatment yet (the mobile phase). The bar only
     wraps, so nothing is cut off or scrolls sideways. There is no fallback to the
     old header and pill.
8. **One shared renderer.** The pages say which area they are and what they are
   about. They do not build a header.

## What the drawings are

They were generated by `00-app-shell.build.mjs` from the walkthrough build of the
pre-migration app (main at 1d14abd), at 1440×900 with the real Fredoka and
Nunito. From each page it removed only the old chrome: the static `<header>` with
its hard-coded Economics unit, the pill switcher, and on Essay practice the old
`header.qp-nav` and what the app left underneath that overlay. It inserted the
bar, and on Essay practice the area's own row, and proved:

1. **The page under the bar is unchanged.** Every content element keeps its left,
   width and height, and moves by one vertical offset.
2. **The static file renders as the page did,** box for box and pixel for pixel.
3. **There is one bar.** Same geometry on every page. At 1024px, one row with
   every subject it can name shown in full. At 1023px and 861px, one row with the
   subject left out. Each is checked with the sample account and with a
   nine-digit student number, and with the demo badge.
4. **Open states survive** the CSS pruning.

The generator strips the old chrome, so it runs only against a build that still
has it. It is kept as the record of the approved design, not regenerated.

## The bar

- **Frame.** White, a 1px `#E8EEEF` bottom border, sticky. Inner column 1320px
  maximum with 28px side padding. 67.4px tall.
- **Brand.** A 30px `#12B27A` tile with a white Fredoka "M", then "Marginal" in
  Fredoka 700 at 18px. It goes to the Study home.
- **Areas.** Nunito 600 14px `#63787F`. The current one is 700 `#18272D` with a
  `#12B27A` underline and `aria-current="page"`. Each link reserves its bold
  width, so the bar never shifts between pages.
- **Right side.** The subject (13px 600 `#63787F`), the demo badge on Essay
  practice's demo route, a divider, the account (a user icon, class code ·
  student number) and Sign out. With cloud sign-in off there is no account and
  no Sign out.
  - The subject is what shortens if a label is ever longer than the room. The
    account never shortens while a subject can give way; it has a 28ch ceiling and
    keeps its full value as a title.
  - Sign out has a transparent 1px border, so hover does not move it, and a −14px
    right margin, so its text ends at the content edge.
- **Focus.** A 2px `#0E9765` ring on the brand, every link and Sign out.
- **Essay practice's row.** `Essay practice | My essays` in the page under the
  bar, aligned to the brand, 14px `#5D7178`, the current one 700 with the green
  underline.

## How it is built

- **One host.** `index.html` carries `<header class="ash" id="ash">` holding the
  signed-out bar (the brand alone), so a page has its bar before any script runs.
- **One renderer.** `shellRender()` in app.js draws the bar from one state with
  two inputs: `shellPage(area, context)` from the `#app` pages (Study, Create,
  Test mode, the sign-in page), and `shellEssay(state)` from Essay practice, an
  overlay over one of those. Essay practice's state wins while it is open; when it
  closes the bar falls back to the page underneath. `shellSubHTML()` draws Essay
  practice's row from the same block.
- **Routes are the old controls' routes.** The brand goes to the Study home and
  Study to `home()`, as the old header and pill did. Leaving Essay practice goes
  through `esLeave`, which saves the draft. Essay practice's own link, when it is
  already open, takes the area's own "Essay practice" route. Sign out leaves Essay
  practice and Test mode first.
- **The overlays start below the bar** (`.qp`, `.es-scrim`: `inset:
  var(--ash-h) 0 0`, the bar's measured height). The writing toolbar keeps Exit
  essay, the area's two links, Learn, Notebook, the mode switch, Setup and its
  More menu; the brand, subject and badge moved to the bar.
- **Allowances for the old chrome follow the bar.** The sitting's `min-height`, the
  narrow-screen drawer's `top` and the floating study and tool windows measure from
  the bar, or from Essay practice's surface under it, not from the top of the window.
- **Where scrollbars take up room** (Windows, or "always show scrollbars"), a
  one-time probe adds `html.ash-gutter`, which keeps the gutter on the page and on
  Essay practice's surfaces. The centred bar then never moves sideways between a
  page that scrolls and one that does not, and Essay practice's row stays under the
  brand. Overlay scrollbars take no room and nothing changes. The cost there: a page
  too short to scroll sits half a scrollbar further left than it did.
- **Guards the bar made necessary.** Study's keyboard shortcuts ignore keys meant
  for the bar or for Essay practice opened over a card. A marking reply for a
  Study card the student has left by the bar is dropped rather than written into
  a session that is gone.

## Checked

- **Content under the bar, before and after, on 15 pages.** The pre-migration
  build and the implementation were captured on Study (home, a topic, its modes,
  a card), Create, Test mode (library, import, paper, question type, sitting) and
  Essay practice (setup, My essays, question list, coached writing, full
  attempt). Every content element keeps its left, width and height and moves by
  one vertical offset per group. The one intended difference is the sitting: it
  now fills the window under the bar, so its footer sits on the bottom edge as
  before (within 0.22px), where the old 140px allowance would have left it 44px
  above it.
- **ui78** (full tier, 197 assertions): one bar and nothing of the old chrome on
  13 pages; the current area; the subject on each page, including a writing screen
  whose attempt differs from the picker; Essay practice's row; account and Sign out
  on every page; Sign out from Essay practice and Test mode, with the notebook
  open; the signed-out bar equals index.html's; 1024px, 1023px and 861px; the
  bar's CSS is this drawing's and its pixels match it; Study's keys; a late marking
  reply; the landmarks; where the floating windows open; Essay practice switched
  off; a question imported after the app opened; classic scrollbars.
- **t30**: Study's key resolves to the package Study's content names, and nothing
  outside the shell reads the bar's state.
- **Mutations**: 26 catalogue entries (tools/mutations.js, `shell-` and
  `study-subject-key-dropped`), each killed by its owning assertion.
- **Adversarial review**: five reviewers and a skeptic per finding. Seven
  findings were confirmed and are fixed above; seven were refuted as pre-existing
  or not reproducible.

## Behaviour worth knowing

- **Leaving Essay practice by an area link goes to that area's page.** Study goes
  to the Study home or the topic the student was in, Test mode to its library. The
  old picker's Home returned to whatever page was under the overlay, and Exit essay
  on a writing screen still does.

## Still open

1. **Mobile.** At 860px and below the bar only wraps. The consolidated mobile
   phase designs it.
2. **Window title.** `index.html`'s title still joins "Marginal" and the Economics
   unit with an em dash, on every page. It should follow the area.
3. **Surfaces outside the app.** The teacher tools and the importer are separate
   pages with their own headers.
4. **Em dashes in page copy.** Some Study and Create text joins its words with an
   em dash. That copy is the app's own and is not swept here.
