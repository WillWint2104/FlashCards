Question 15· 20 marks extended response · evaluate

Evaluate the effectiveness of marketing strategies in achieving the marketing objectives of a business you have studied.

Your submitted response· 176 words

Northline Sportswear set a marketing objective of increasing its share of the teenage market, and most of its strategies were chosen to serve that objective. The business moved from print advertising to short video content on social platforms, which reached the age group it was targeting at a lower cost per view than its previous campaigns.

Its second strategy was a loyalty app that gave members early access to new releases. This was effective because it turned occasional buyers into repeat customers, and repeat customers are cheaper to keep than new ones are to find.

However, the business also cut its prices across the range at the same time, which worked against the premium position its advertising was building. A customer who sees the brand as aspirational and then finds it discounted every month will stop believing the first message.

Overall the marketing strategies were effective in achieving the objective they were set, because the business gained share in the group it targeted, but the pricing decision limited how much of that gain it could hold.

What the marker noticed

Where the marker could point at the sentence it meant, your own words are shown. Where it could not, the observation is about the response as a whole.

  1. The comparison is stated but never quantified

    In your response
    The business moved from print advertising to short video content on social platforms, which reached the age group it was targeting at a lower cost per view than its previous campaigns

    You say the cost per view was lower than the previous campaigns. Lower by how much, and over what period? A figure here would turn the claim into evidence.

  2. Effectiveness is asserted, and the reason given is general rather than about this business

    In your response
    This was effective because it turned occasional buyers into repeat customers, and repeat customers are cheaper to keep than new ones are to find

    Retention being cheaper than acquisition is true of businesses generally. What would make it evidence is what happened at Northline after the app launched.

  3. The strongest paragraph, and the one closest to an evaluation

    In your response
    However, the business also cut its prices across the range at the same time, which worked against the premium position its advertising was building

    You set two strategies against each other and say why they conflict. This is the reasoning the rest of the response needs more of.

  4. The judgement arrives only at the end

    Across your response

    Each paragraph reaches its own small verdict and the position that ties them together appears in the last three lines. Stating it first would make the response read as one argument rather than four.

  5. The objective is set but never measured

    Across your response

    The response is judged against achieving a marketing objective, so the one number that would settle it is the change in share.
