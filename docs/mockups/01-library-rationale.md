## What each part of the page is for

- **Header.** No paper is open, so the right-hand label is *Test mode* and no
  subject is named. The paper's subject appears in the header once it is opened
  (decision 4, and the P0 in decision 18). There is no Economics fallback,
  because there is no fallback at all.
- **Site navigation.** The app's own four tabs, in the shared tokens. Test Mode
  is a place on the website, not a separate application.
- **Page head.** It says what Practice means in one sentence, with the policy pill
  the shell already uses. **Import a paper** lives here: Test Mode owns exam import.
- **Paper card, three columns.**
  - Who the paper is: course and stage from the curriculum block, the title (a
    link to the overview), and questions, sections, marks and time from the
    contract.
  - Where the attempt stands: the status and its counts.
  - What to do next: one filled button, at most one outlined button, and
    **Delete paper** as an underlined text control beneath them. Delete is
    reachable and never the same weight as Resume. It turns coral on hover and
    asks for confirmation.

## Decided here, open to change on review

- **Selected sections** appear as a line under the facts (*"Sections I and II
  only"*) only when the attempt is not the whole paper. None of the example
  attempts is partial, so it is described here and not drawn.
- **Not marked** joins the count line only when it is above zero.
- **Completed shows the mark, not a percentage or a band.** The mark is what the
  paper is out of.
- **Try again keeps the last result.** A paper being retried shows In progress.
  Whether its card should also show the last completed mark is left for review,
  and not drawn.

## What the numbers reveal, and why they are lower than a student would expect

The marks are derived through the shipped code, so they carry two live marking
defects found while deriving them (UX-TEST-18, -19):

- 11(a), 11(b) and 12(a) score 0 for correct answers, because a weighted point is
  matched against the text of its own description.
- The calculation answer `1.5 : 1` scores only because its digits merge into
  1.51, within the tolerance.

The completed 59/90 would be higher with both fixed. The page's design does not
depend on the value. The marking does, and that is a Slice A correctness item.
