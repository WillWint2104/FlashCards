📄 Kerbside CoffeeShared source · Question 11

Kerbside Coffee roasts and sells coffee from one site and four vans. Demand at the vans peaks between 7am and 9am, and the owners have had complaints about waiting times.

Last year the business bought a second roaster using a three-year loan. Staff turnover among van operators is high, and most operators are employed casually.
