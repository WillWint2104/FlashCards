# Slice B, state 1: Submit paper. Reading key

**For approval.** Desktop only, at 1440 and 1280×900 (decision 23). No
question format and no Slice A page is redrawn. Generated by
`04-submit.build.mjs` from `04-submit.fixture.json`, which holds only what a
student did. Every status, count and mark on these pages is read from
`ATT.report` in `tools/contract/attempts.js`, the same function the page will
call. A marker failure's words are read out of app.js, never retyped.

| file | how the student got here | the page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `04-submit.html` | Finish paper on the last question, whole paper | **28/90**: 13 answered and marked, 2 not marked, 5 not answered, 3 flagged, Section IV not chosen (the primary proposal) |
| `04-submit-marking.html` | the same, with Question 12(b) just sent for marking | a blue notice with **Leave it unmarked**; Submit paper is disabled and says why |
| `04-submit-section.html` | Section II only, started after a completed whole-paper attempt | **16/40** for Section II; the last result (67/90) and *Submitting replaces this result* |
| `04-submit-complete.html` | everything answered and marked | **67/90**, all 20 answered and marked; nothing to go back to except a flag |
| `04-submit-practice.html` | Finish practice on a short-answer session | the same page for a practice session (proposed): *Finish practice*, no sections, no either/or |

## What the page is for

Desktop v1 is Practice only (decision 17). Every answer was marked when it was
submitted, so submitting the paper marks nothing more. It closes the attempt
on the results it already has, and the results open next. A student needs to
know, in a few seconds, whether submitting now loses anything, and where to go
if it does.

So the page is the decision first and the detail after it:

1. **The decision.** *Submitting ends this attempt with the marks you have
   now*, and the policy in one sentence: *Answers are marked as you submit
   them, so submitting the paper marks nothing more.* It is worded as the
   policy, not as a claim that every answer here was marked, because some
   were not.
2. **What you are submitting.** Three rows that add up to the attempt:
   *Answered and marked*, *Submitted, not marked*, *Not answered*, each with
   its questions, what they are worth, and what they earned. The total row is
   the attempt: *The whole paper · 20 · 90 marks · 28/90 marks so far*.
3. **Notes**, one line each: what not-marked and not-answered questions mean
   for the total, how many are flagged, and any answer changed after marking.
4. **The detail.** One list per thing a student may want to go back to, each
   row with its own route back.
5. **By section**, Page 3's table, for a paper.
6. **The action**, in Page 3's sticky bar: one sentence naming what is lost,
   and one filled button.

The aside, *About this attempt*, holds the scope: the sections chosen, each
either/or with the paper's own instruction and where the student stands on
it, and when the attempt was started and saved.

The page itself is the confirmation. The `confirm()` dialog that Finish paper
shows today goes.

## How each requirement is met

- **Answered and marked, submitted but not marked, unanswered: distinct.**
  They are three rows of one table and three groups of rows below it. One
  predicate decides an item's status (the outcome the tally uses), so the
  table, the lists, the bar's sentence and the By section table cannot
  disagree. The build fails if the three rows do not add up to the attempt's
  total and maximum, or if they disagree with `ATT.summary`.
- **Flagged: distinct, and never added in.** Flags describe questions already
  counted, so they sit under the table, not in it, and in their own list where
  each flagged question shows its own status. *Flags do not change a mark, and
  they do not stop you submitting.*
- **A marker failure never reads as zero, or as marking done.** A not-marked
  row's Earned reads *Not marked*; a not-answered row's reads *None*. No
  cell reads 0 (the build checks it). Every not-marked answer shows the
  shipped reason verbatim, and what could change it:
  - *marking it again can help*: the marker failed (busy, unreachable, a
    reply it could not read). Route: *Go to 12(a) to try marking again*.
  - *changing the answer can help*: a calculation written so it cannot be
    read. Route: *Go to 11(c) to change your answer*.
  - *your teacher can fix this in Settings*: no marker connected, or the
    class code not accepted. The shipped reason says what to do.
  - *cannot be marked here*: a refusal nothing changes, such as a question
    whose answer key is incomplete. *Marking it again here will not change
    this.*
- **Unmarked and unanswered marks stay in the total.** *Questions not marked
  or not answered earn nothing when you submit. Their 55 marks still count in
  the 90.*
- **The either/or is never presented as requiring both.** While Section IV is
  not chosen, it is one row, *Question 15 or 16 · 20 marks · Not chosen yet.
  Answer one of these, not both. It counts as one question*, counted once in
  the 20 and the 90. The aside quotes the paper's instruction under *Original
  paper instructions*. Once a question is chosen and written in, the other is
  named as *not part of this attempt* and appears in no row or count.
- **Section scope.** The facts line names it as Page 3 does: *The whole
  paper* or *Section II only*, with its questions and marks. The aside lists
  the sections and says they are fixed once an attempt starts. Sections not
  chosen appear nowhere, never as not answered.
- **Marks earned so far.** The total row and the bar: *28/90*.
- **Routes back** to every not-answered, not-marked and flagged question, and
  to any answer changed after marking. Each link names the question and what
  to do there. The back link at the top returns to where the student was.
- **Marking still in flight blocks submission.** While an answer is being
  marked, a blue notice names it (blue already means *in progress* here; gold
  is flags only). It is tagged *Being marked now* (or *Being marked again*
  for a retry) in the notice and in its list alike, with no route that would
  send it for marking a second time, and each row of the table counts what in
  it is being marked. Submit paper is a real disabled button, and the bar says
  why: *Question 12(b) is still being marked. You can submit when it has its
  mark, or leave it unmarked.* The notice has *Leave it unmarked* with its
  consequence spelled out. When the mark lands, the page updates in place and
  Submit paper is enabled. If focus was inside the notice when it closes
  (*Leave it unmarked*, or the last mark arriving), it moves to Submit paper;
  otherwise it stays where it is. The bar's sentence is a polite live region
  whose text changes, so the change is announced.
- **A second opinion in flight blocks submission too.** *What would make this
  stronger* sends a marked answer to the marker again. Today nothing tracks it
  as pending, so submitting would throw its reply away. When the page is
  built, those requests are tracked in their own in-memory record (not the
  submission one, whose guard would discard the second opinion's own reply),
  and the notice reads *A second opinion on Question 11(b) is still being
  marked. Your mark of 3 of 3 marks stands until it arrives.*
- **Being marked is never shown after a reload.** It is the app's in-memory
  record, never stored. After a reload, a first submission that had not come
  back is a draft again (*Draft saved · written, not submitted*), with a route
  to submit it. The build checks this.

- **Changes after marking** are listed only where the sitting shows them: a
  written answer rewritten after *Try again* and not resubmitted (its earlier
  mark stands), or an unmarked answer edited and not resubmitted (it stays not
  marked). A change still being marked was submitted, so it is not listed. A
  multiple-choice re-pick is not listed, because the sitting does not show it
  beside the mark.
- **A practice session that holds both options of an either/or** lists them
  as two questions, as decision 22 approved for the bank: in practice each
  option is practised on its own.

## Contract added for this page

- `ATT.report(attempt, exams, pending)`: per item, status (`marked`,
  `not_marked`, `not_answered`), marks, weight, the shipped reason, what could
  help, draft, changed after marking, pending, flagged; the three rows with
  counts, worth and earned; flags, pending and changed keys; by section; and
  each either/or (chosen, options, locked). Tested in `tests/t38.mjs`: the rows
  add up to `summary()`, and every classification above.
- `ATT.sane` now writes an explicit outcome onto every stored result, so
  `isMarked()` and the tally can never read one result two ways (a gap the
  design review found).
- `ATT.choose` now carries a flag along with an either/or choice. A flag set
  on *Question 15 or 16*, or on the option not taken, moves to the question
  chosen (its first part, when it has parts). Before this, choosing silently
  dropped it from every count and from this page. This one changes Slice A's
  sitting, as a fix; tested in `tests/t38.mjs`.

Nothing in the app calls `ATT.report` yet. The page is not built.

## For decision

1. **Leave it unmarked.** Recommended rule: it never removes a mark.
   - A first submission is recorded as submitted, not marked, with the reason
     *This response was not marked: you chose not to wait for its mark.*
   - A resubmission keeps the earlier mark; the new answer is not marked.
   - A retry of a not-marked answer stays not marked.
   - In every case a mark that arrives later is not used.

   This needs one new attempt operation and one reason code. The alternative,
   needing no new code, is to stop waiting and leave the text as a draft, so
   it is *not answered* rather than *not marked*.
2. **Finish practice uses this page** (`04-submit-practice.html`), with
   *Finishing* in place of *Submitting*, no sections, no either/or, and an
   aside saying it changes no paper attempt. Or does practice keep a lighter
   confirmation?
3. **One verb for a paper.** The sitting's last question says *Finish paper*;
   this page says *Submit paper*. Recommended: the footer label becomes
   *Submit paper*, a copy change only. And is the last question the only way
   here, or should the navigator also offer it? Recommended: the last question
   only, for v1.
4. **After submitting, not-marked answers stay not marked.** Decision 23 closes
   the attempt on the results already obtained, so Results and Review will not
   offer *Try marking again*. The page says *you can review your marks but not
   change them*. Confirm.
5. **Submitting with nothing answered** is allowed, and the bar says what is
   lost (*… at 0/90, with 20 questions not answered*). Where a completed
   result exists, the page shows it with *Submitting replaces this result*,
   because v1 keeps only the latest completed attempt.

## Not drawn, checked by the build

- A refusal a setting fixes reads *your teacher can fix this in Settings*,
  never *cannot be marked here*; one nothing changes reads *cannot be marked
  here*, with no retry. Both with the app's own reasons.
- A retry still being marked offers no second retry, and is counted as being
  marked on its row.
- Nothing is being marked after a reload.
- A chosen either/or with work in it is locked, and its other option is in no
  list.
- The complete attempt has nothing not marked and nothing not answered.
