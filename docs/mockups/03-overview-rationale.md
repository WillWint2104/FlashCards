## One shell, two ways in, never one attempt

Both are the same page.

- **The page.** The same header, back link, title block and policy pill, then
  two columns: what you will sit, with the only filled button, and what to
  know before you do.
- **A paper** offers its sections. Start paper begins
  `attempts["paper:" + exam.id]`.
- **A type** offers its questions. Start practice begins
  `attempts["type:" + format]`.

Both open the same sitting (state 8), with the same navigator, marking,
feedback and saving. They share no attempt state (decision 19). The type page
says so with a real example from the bank: *Answering Question 11(a) here does
not answer it in your attempt at Business Studies practice paper*.

## A paper

- **The header names the subject** the way the sitting will. It comes from the
  paper's own curriculum, resolved against the packages Marginal has: *Business
  Studies / Test mode* (decision 4, the P0 header fix). Page 1 names no subject,
  because no paper is open there.
- **Where a student starts, the paper says what it is.** The workflow audit
  found the instructions were never shown. The course and time appeared only
  on the library row, never where a student starts the paper.
  - The course is the kicker.
  - The instructions are under *About this paper*, as written, marked *From the
    paper*.
  - The time is under *Time*, not in the facts line as on Pages 1 and 2,
    because here it needs its sentence: *Practice is not timed, so use the
    paper's times as a guide to pace* (decision 17).
- **Each section is one large target.** The whole row is the label for its
  box. The section's own instructions are shown. Their opening *40 marks.* is
  dropped only when it says exactly what the row already shows.
- **The either/or** shows the paper's own instruction (*Attempt EITHER Question
  15 OR Question 16*) and *choose 1 of 2*, as Page 2's contents do. The page
  does not say when or how the choice is made. That is settled with the sitting
  and state 19, not here.
- **The counts are the app's own.** `PAPER.totals` per section, as the current
  picker and Page 1 count them. An either/or counts once.
- **The summary names sections by their own names.** One section reads
  *Section II only*. Several read *2 of 4 sections*, with the rows showing
  which. All read *All 4 sections*. A name is the section's authored name up to
  its dash, and a section with no name is numbered as the app numbers it.
  Nothing is numbered from its position.
- **Section practice lives here,** not in a tile (decision 20). Ticking only
  Section II is how a student practises Section II as a paper.
- **Select all and Clear** are kept from the current picker. With nothing
  ticked, Start paper is a disabled button: it cannot be clicked, focused or
  pressed with Enter. The total reads *Choose at least one section to start*.
  That state is not a separate page: press Clear on any of them.

## In progress

- **The same numbers as Page 1's card,** from the same derivation: 12 of 20
  answered, 2 flagged, 13/90 marks so far, saved 29 Sep, picks up at Question
  11(c).
- **By section,** each row is tallied from that section's own questions, by
  the same `tally` that counts the paper. The column reads *Marks so far*.
  - A section where nothing has been submitted reads *Not started*.
  - One whose only answers the marker could not mark reads *Not marked yet*
    (decision 20), never *Not started*.
- **Which sections** are named only when the attempt is not the whole paper,
  as Page 1's card does. *Sections are fixed once an attempt starts* is always
  said. Starting again mid-attempt is not offered. See *For decision*.

## Try again

Page 1's completed card leads here. The last result sits above the choice in
its own region (*Your last attempt*, a heading for screen readers) and does not
constrain the choice. It names its sections when it was not the whole paper.
*Those results stay available until you submit the new attempt.* That is the
attempt rule: Try again starts a new `current` and keeps `last`
(docs/testmode-attempt-state.md). Where they are reached while the new attempt
is in progress is under *Not drawn*.

## A question type

- **The bank follows Page 1's tile rule.** It counts by canonical format: parts
  count, parents do not, and both options of an either/or count. Here that is
  the four short answers 11(a), 11(b), 12(a) and 12(b). The extended responses
  inside Section II are not here, and the page says why: *Questions are grouped
  by how they are answered, not by the section they are in.*
- **Each question shows its full prompt, its marks and what it refers to.**
  That is every resource the sitting shows with it, in the sitting's order: the
  section's source material, the parent's sources, then the question's own.
  - The first is named by its caption, or by the sitting's own label (*Source*,
    *Source 2*) when it has none. The rest are counted: *Table 1: Selected
    financial data, Harbourline Cycles, and 1 more source*.
  - Body text is never printed as a name.
- **Paper order,** always. There is no shuffling.
- **All or choose.** *All 4 questions* is the default.
  - *Choose questions* shows a box on each row, with Select all and Clear.
  - Going back to All keeps the picks, so returning to Choose finds them.
  - The total and Start practice follow the choice. With nothing chosen, Start
    practice is disabled, as Start paper is.
- **One question offers no choice,** so it has none. Page 1's Calculations and
  Business report tiles each hold one, and their page shows the question and
  Start practice with no All, Choose or box.
- **The header** names the subject when every source paper shares one. With
  papers from two subjects it reads *Test mode* alone.

## Behaviour the pages carry

- **The live totals are the build's own functions.** The summary a variant is
  drawn with is computed by a function whose source is written into the page.
  The page runs that same function when a box changes, so the static state
  and the live one cannot count differently.
- **The action bar sticks to the bottom of the window** while the panel is in
  view, so a bank of twenty questions cannot push Start off the screen. At
  1280×900 every variant's primary action is on the first screen.
- **A box focused from the keyboard scrolls clear of the bar.**
- **The 1440 captures are whole pages with the bar unstuck,** so they show the
  panel as it scrolls out. The 1280 captures are the first screen as a student
  sees it, bar and all.

## Checked by the build, not drawn

These branches have no variant of their own, so the build checks them every
time it runs and fails if one is wrong:

- a one-question bank, which must offer no choice;
- a section whose only answer was refused by the marker, which reads *Not
  marked yet*;
- a section's own source, which comes first;
- an uncaptioned source, which is named *Source* and never by its text;
- a summary for one section, or one question, which is never *1 sections* or
  *1 of 1 questions*, and names the section by its own name.

Each check was proved by breaking the code it guards and watching the build
fail with that check's message.

## Not drawn, and where each goes

- **Marking not connected.** The app's existing line moves into *How practice
  works*, with the count that matters: *9 of these 20 questions need the
  marker. Until your teacher connects marking, their answers stay unmarked.*
  The count is the chosen sections' questions that `PAPER.markerDependent`
  names. The paper has 10 such questions, but Questions 15 and 16 are an
  either/or, so a sitting meets 9.
- **An attempt on an older version** (decision 21). The attempt panel adds:
  *This attempt is on version 1. Your library now has version 2, which your
  next attempt will use.* The attempt panel and the by-section table read the
  pinned version. The title block and *About this paper* read the library's.
- **The last result while a new attempt is in progress.** The *Your attempt*
  panel gains one line above its counts: *Last completed 30 Sep · 67 / 90*,
  with View results. This is where Try again's promise is kept.
- **A type session in progress.** Resume practice on a tile goes straight to
  the saved question, as Resume paper does on Page 1, so this page is not on
  that path. Each format holds one session at a time.
- **A type with nothing available.** Page 1's tile reads *None available yet*
  and has no Start, so this page is never reached empty.

## For decision

1. **Choosing questions, not a number.** You described *choose how many or use
   all*. The page lets a student pick which questions, which also sets how
   many. A bare number needs a rule for which questions it means, and no
   random draw has been decided. If you want a number, say whether it takes
   the first N in paper order or a random N.
2. **Starting again while an attempt is in progress.** Not offered, for a paper
   or a type. A student who wants different sections or questions has to
   submit first. The alternatives:
   - *Start again*, which discards the attempt in progress after a
     confirmation that says how many answers it holds;
   - adding sections to the attempt in progress.
3. **The paper's instructions are shown as written** even when one section is
   chosen, so *Attempt all questions in Sections I to III* stays on the page
   during Section II practice. It is labelled as the paper's, not Marginal's.

## Found in Page 1, which stays frozen

Page 1 is not changed. These are recorded for when it reopens, or for the
Slice A implementation, whichever comes first:

- **The tile counts must use the import door's test.** Page 3's bank counts a
  paper as sittable by `examine()` with the packages (decision 21). Page 1's
  generator predates that and calls `examine()` without them. The counts agree
  on every paper drawn, but they would differ for a paper whose subject
  Marginal cannot mark.
- **Page 1's scope line numbers sections from their position** (*Sections II
  only* for a single section). Page 3 names them from the paper.
- **Page 1's in-progress card does not reach the last result.** Page 3's
  attempt panel is where it goes (see *Not drawn*).

## Content changed for this page

The synthetic paper's instructions said they were *written to exercise the exam
contract*, words for a developer that Page 3 would show a student. They now
read as a paper's author would write them: *Attempt all questions in Sections I
to III, and one question in Section IV. This is an original practice paper. It
is not a past HSC examination, and none of its questions are taken from one.*
The old *Attempt all questions* also contradicted Section IV's either/or.
`02-import-notjson.html`, which cuts the file in half, regenerated with it.
