# Slice B, state 2: Results overview. Reading key

**For approval.** Desktop only, at 1440 and 1280×900 (decision 24). No question
format and no Slice A or Submit page is redrawn. Generated by
`05-results.build.mjs` from `05-results.fixture.json`, which says only which
closed attempt each page shows. Each attempt is replayed through the shipped
contract (`replay.mjs`, shared with the Submit generator) and closed with
`ATT.complete`, as Submit paper closes it. Every mark, count and word on these
pages is read from `ATT.results`, which tallies `ATT.report`'s own items, so
Results cannot disagree with Review & submit.

## The page: the paper as a map of its marks

A 90-mark paper is readable at a glance because the whole structure is on the
first screen at 1280×900 (the build measures it: the last section ends at
881px):

1. **The mark and what it is made of.** *Your mark: 28 / 90*, with *13 of 20
   answered · 2 not marked · ⚑ 3 flagged*, beside Submit's own table (Answered
   and marked, Submitted not marked, Not answered, and the total row), and the
   one filled button, *Review each question*.
2. **The map.** One band per section, in paper order, with its subtotal on one
   right-hand edge. Parent questions stay groups with their parts beneath them
   and their own subtotal (*Question 11 · 5 / 14*, with the case study's
   caption, verbatim). Every answerable is a cell holding its own mark or the
   words for why it has none. Every cell opens that question's review.
3. **Below the fold:** what to look at again (Submit's lists, in Submit's order)
   and *About this attempt*.

## Never zero for work with no mark

- A cell with no mark says *Not marked* or *Not answered*, with what it was
  worth (*c · 4 marks*). Only a marked cell shows numbers; a marked 0 (*Q4 0 /
  1*) is a mark like any other.
- A group or section has three words: its mark (*5 / 40*, including a real
  *0 / 14*), *Nothing marked* (something was submitted and none of it was
  marked), or *Not answered* (nothing was submitted).
- The total row and the score read *Nothing marked* when nothing was, never
  *0 / 90*.
- *Questions not marked or not answered earned nothing. Their 55 marks still
  count in the 90.*
- Cells follow the navigator's chips: a dashed edge is not answered, a solid
  dark edge is not marked, a light edge is marked. Each cell also says it in
  words, so colour and line are never the only signal. The table's swatches are
  the legend.

## Parts are never flattened

Question 11 and Question 12 are groups, each with its caption, its parts as
cells, its subtotal and *2 of 4 parts answered · 1 not marked · 1 not
answered*. In a practice session a parent is out of the parts in the session
(*Question 11 · 5 / 5 · 2 of its 4 parts were in this practice*), never the
parent's 14. Two papers' 11(a) are two bands, never merged (`ATT.results`
keys groups by the paper's own id; tested in `tests/t38.mjs`).

## The either/or

Once: *Q15 or Q16 · 20 marks · Not answered*, with *Neither question was
chosen. It counts as one question.* When one was chosen: *You chose Question 15.
Question 16 was not part of this attempt.*, and Question 16 has no cell and no
count. The aside quotes the paper under *Original paper instructions*.

## A closed attempt

- Nothing on the page offers *Try again*, *Try marking again*, or a route that
  would mark anything (decision 24). The words *yet* and *so far* do not
  appear (the build checks).
- A not-marked answer shows the reason recorded at the time, verbatim, after
  *Reason given at the time:*, under *This attempt is closed, so they stay not
  marked, and any step a reason suggests applies only to a new attempt.*
- *Start new attempt* (*Start practice*) is the one ghost button; while a new
  attempt is in progress it is replaced by the blue strip and *Resume paper*.
- The aside says the attempt is closed, when it was started and completed, and
  that only the latest completed attempt is kept.

## Not on the page

Percentage, band, grade, comparison with anyone, time taken, predicted mark,
the sitting's mood labels (*Most of it*, *Partly there*), *Correct* / *Not
quite*, *Solution viewed*, marker summaries, criteria and prompt text. The data
cannot back the first six; the rest belong to individual-question review
(state 3).

## Contract added for this page

`ATT.results(attempt, exams)`: `ATT.report` plus bands (paper and section),
parent groups with the parts in the attempt and their caption, a tally and one
of the three words for each, the outcome per item (failed or refused), the
dates, and the version the attempt was on against the library's. Tested in
`tests/t38.mjs`. Nothing in the app calls it yet.

## For decision

1. **Results and Review together.** Every cell and *Review each question* open
   state 3, which is not designed yet, and Submit promises *your results open
   next, where you can review your marks*. Recommended: design Review next and
   build Results and Review together, so every link works on the first day.
   The alternative is to ship Results first with cells that do not open.
2. **One meaning for a dashed edge.** Results follows the navigator (dashed is
   not answered). Submit's frozen tags use dashed for not marked. Recommended:
   a one-line style change to Submit's tag so the whole of Test mode agrees.
3. **"Nothing marked" elsewhere.** A completed attempt with nothing marked
   still reads *0 / 90* on the library card, Page 3's last-attempt strip and
   Submit's last-result strip. Recommended: those three say *Nothing marked*,
   as Results does.
4. **Reaching a practice session's results again.** Once Results is left, a
   finished practice session's result cannot be found. Recommended: the
   question type's tile or overview shows *Last completed 3 Oct · 5 / 13 ·
   View results*.
5. **Reasons that suggest a next step** (*try marking it again*, *Write the
   final value...*, *Settings*) are quoted verbatim under the closed lede
   (recommended), rather than cut.

## Not drawn, checked by the build

- A parent whose marked parts all earned 0 reads *0 / 14*, not *Nothing marked*.
- A superseded version is named against the library's.
- An either/or chosen and left empty reads *You chose Question 15 and did not
  answer it*.
- Every band and group adds up to its section and to the attempt, and agrees
  with `ATT.summary`.
